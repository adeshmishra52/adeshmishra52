## 📊 Evidence & Analytics Portfolio

### [When Averages Mislead: Diagnostic Gaps in Education Programme Management](https://github.com/adeshmishra52/ibm-portfolio)

A programme-level evidence note built from live data: **312 students across 14 Delhi NCR centres** (JEE Main 2026, Session 1). It shows how headline averages hide the signals that matter.

| Finding | What the data showed |
|---------|----------------------|
| **Mean–median trap** | A 9.9-point gap exposed a struggling lower tail that headline reporting did not show |
| **Resource-allocation paradox** | Physics (r = 0.90, 81% of variance) predicted outcomes better than Maths (r = 0.66), even though Physics was the strongest subject |
| **Centre-level variance** | Centres with the same curriculum had a 4× difference in JEE Advanced conversion |
| **Recognition failure** | 35 students crossed the 99th percentile, 2.5× more than the 14 formally recognised |

The repository also includes a case study on building a 14-centre education practice with 1,500+ learners a year: curriculum architecture, a from-scratch LMS (+25% online enrolment) and topic-level diagnostics (~15% YoY pass-rate improvement).
